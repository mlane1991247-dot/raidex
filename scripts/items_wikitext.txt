'''Looting''' is the main source for acquiring items, gear and weapons in the rust belt. These items are needed for [[Quests]], [[Raider Den|Workshop Upgrades]], Crafting or can be sold or recycled to break them down into smaller resources. Recycling items during a raid only returns a portion of the usual components.

Use the categorized tables below to prioritize what to take and what to leave behind.
